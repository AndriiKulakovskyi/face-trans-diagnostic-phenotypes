{\sffamily\bfseries\color{faceaccent}\small ABSTRACT}\par\vspace{5pt}
{\small
A psychiatric diagnosis predicts the \emph{average} patient in a class, not the individual in the room: bipolar
disorder, schizophrenia, and major depression each aggregate biologically and prognostically heterogeneous
people under one label. \textbf{FACE-ATLAS} asks what is recovered when the harmonized three-cohort FACE
baseline ($N=9{,}013$) is reorganized around \emph{continuous, diagnosis-agnostic latent dimensions} instead of
categories. The answer is a single coherent argument resting on \textbf{three pillars}.\par\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{(I)~Dimensional analysis recovers a map the categories hide.} One global, missingness-aware Bayesian
sparse bifactor/ESEM model --- fit to each patient's \emph{observed cells only} (no imputation, full sample),
diagnosis held out as \emph{metadata}, axes discovered on baseline --- resolves a \textbf{nine-dimension
transdiagnostic map}: a general functional-burden factor $G$ plus eight weakly-correlated axes (cognition,
metabolic, inflammatory, sleep, developmental-risk, suicidality, mania, substance). Its load-bearing payoff is
a \textbf{three-way dissociation --- biology $\perp$ symptoms $\perp$ severity}: metabolic and inflammatory load
correlate with the severity factor $G$ at only $0.12$ and $0.07$ (versus $0.39$ for cognition, $0.42$ for
sleep), sit at the $\approx 0.08$ floor against the symptom axes, and survive adjustment for medication,
adiposity, and site --- so two patients who are equally ill \emph{and} equally symptomatic can still differ
sharply in biological load. The map is transdiagnostic (adjusted Rand $\approx 0$ vs.\ DSM-5) and a
\emph{tighter} description of the patients than diagnosis.\par\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{(II)~Archetypes make the map interpretable and clinically legible.} On these coordinates the space is a
graded \textbf{continuum, not discrete biotypes} (a single-Gaussian falsification null reproduces the apparent
clustering: best silhouette $0.140$ vs.\ $0.141\pm0.002$). Its shape is read through \textbf{four extreme
phenotypes} --- an $A{=}4$ archetype simplex in which biology, symptoms, and severity vary nearly independently
and every patient is a soft blend. The corners compress a nine-vector into a handful of weights a clinician can
say aloud, and make the key dissociation pointable: the biological corner is equally ill yet biologically
opposite to a non-biological severe corner.\par\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{(III)~Operating in this latent space pays off --- with honestly drawn bounds.} Scored forward onto
follow-up without re-estimation, the geometry \emph{persists}: \emph{durable biology} (metabolic test--retest
$\text{ICC}=0.91$), \emph{moving symptoms}. It \emph{forecasts} two-year \textbf{functioning} beyond diagnosis,
severity, and the baseline outcome (archetypes $\Delta\text{ELPD}=+59$; functional remission $27\%\!\to\!60\%$
across the corners; co-informative with DSM-5), and it is a \emph{sufficient, faithful} compression of the
$143$ raw indicators. The bounds are stated as plainly as the gains: the individual-level remission increment is
small ($\Delta\text{AUC}=+0.011$), the signal is course-dependent, and on observational treatment-as-usual the
map does \emph{not} reliably moderate treatment (lithium in bipolar disorder, the cleanest contrast, is a
\emph{well-powered null}).\par\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{The deliverable} is a transdiagnostic biological map that is real, stable, interpretable, and useful at
the \emph{group} level --- demonstrated scientific value plus genuine clinical value as stratification,
continuous forecasting, and monitoring --- with each remaining step to \emph{individual-level} utility named as
a defined next study (incident-event outcomes, randomized treatment data, external validation). A trustworthy
map, and an honest account of how far it reaches.}\par\vspace{7pt}

\pillardivider{I}{Dimensional analysis}{The map the categories hide}{%
\pillarframe
{A small set of continuous, diagnosis-agnostic axes describes these patients better than the diagnostic
label --- and one of them, \emph{biological load}, is nearly invisible to a reading built on diagnosis,
severity, or symptoms alike.}
{One global Bayesian fit on observed cells (no imputation, diagnosis held out) resolves \textbf{nine
transdiagnostic dimensions}. Metabolic and inflammatory load correlate with the severity factor $G$ at just
$0.12$ and $0.07$ (against $0.39$ for cognition and $0.42$ for sleep) \emph{and} sit at the $\approx 0.08$ floor

DSM-5), a tighter description than diagnosis, hardened across six adversarial validation arms, and shipped as
per-patient coordinates carrying honest uncertainty.}
{\emph{Scientifically,} this is the first direct, uncertainty-propagated measurement of how independent
metabolic/inflammatory load is from \emph{both} a transdiagnostic severity axis and the symptom axes.
\emph{Clinically,} it surfaces a risk axis that neither diagnosis, a global-severity impression, nor a symptom
checklist can see --- the foundation everything downstream is built on.}}

\pillardivider{II}{Archetypes}{Reading the continuum without inventing types}{%
\pillarframe
{The map is a graded \emph{continuum}, not a set of biotypes; its shape is best read through four extreme
phenotypes that make the transdiagnostic dissociations pointable and clinically legible --- without
manufacturing false kinds.}
{A single-Gaussian falsification null cannot be separated from the real cloud (best silhouette $0.140$ vs.\
$0.141\pm0.002$). A stability-gated \textbf{$A{=}4$ archetype simplex} separates biology $\perp$ symptoms
$\perp$ severity, with $61\%$ of patients genuine blends rather than members of a type; the biological corner is
\emph{equally ill yet biologically opposite} to a severe non-biological corner. A nested $K$-family is exported
with \textbf{no privileged $K$}.}
{\emph{Scientifically,} it settles the field's continuum-versus-biotype question with a falsification test most
biotype papers skip, and keeps biology a first-class \emph{extreme} no density-gap partition could find.
\emph{Clinically,} it compresses a nine-vector into a handful of weights a clinician can say aloud, and answers
``your most similar patients, across diagnoses, look like this.''}}

\pillardivider{III}{Operating in the latent space}{Persistence, prognosis, treatment --- and the road to utility}{%
\pillarframe
{Working in this latent space delivers value the categorical default cannot reach: a \emph{durable} biological
signal that \emph{forecasts function}, a faithful compression of the raw data, and an honest, \emph{earned}
boundary on treatment with a defined path forward.}
{The measurement holds over follow-up (all five backbone axes invariant) and splits into \emph{durable biology}
(metabolic $\text{ICC}=0.91$) versus \emph{moving symptoms}. The archetype phenotype forecasts two-year
\textbf{functioning} beyond diagnosis $+$ severity $+$ baseline ($\Delta\text{ELPD}=+59$; remission
$27\%\!\to\!60\%$ across the corners; co-informative with DSM-5, $+$both~$+67$), is \emph{sufficient} for
deterioration against all $143$ raw indicators, and its prognostic carrier \emph{survives} adjustment for the
treatments patients received. On observational treatment-as-usual, moderation is a \emph{well-identified null}
(lithium E-value $1.06$).}
{\emph{Scientifically,} \emph{persists}$\to$\emph{predicts} turns a description into a forecast, in a
representation that is transportable and uncertainty-honest. \emph{Clinically,} the map already delivers
group-level stratification, continuous functional forecasting, and a monitoring atlas; the individual-level
calculator and treatment \emph{selection} it does \emph{not} yet deliver define precisely the next studies.}}
