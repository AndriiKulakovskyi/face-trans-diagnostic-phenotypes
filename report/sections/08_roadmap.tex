\section{Future directions}
\label{sec:roadmap}

The results above are internally complete; what remains is a short, principled list of next steps, none of
which changes a conclusion drawn here. They divide into two kinds: methodological follow-ons that would
\emph{tighten} the present estimates, and new studies that would be needed to cross from the scientific
validity demonstrated here to clinical utility in the strong sense.

\subsection{Tightening the present results}
Four loose ends are documented and bounded; each is a refinement, not a change of method.
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item \textbf{Full-precision certification of the explicit-latent block.} The one slow-mixing coupling ---
  the suicidality$\sim$developmental $\Phi$ cell --- has a resample-stable point estimate but a provisional
  precision; a full-$N$, full-precision refit would close it. The continuous backbone and the
  biology$\perp G$ estimand already mix cleanly at full $N$ (cross-seed Tucker $\varphi$ $0.993$).
  \item \textbf{Full-$N$ projection of the non-Gaussian scores.} Extending the suicidality, substance, and
  mania coordinates to all $9{,}013$ patients under the fixed certified parameters --- already validated at
  Pearson $r\approx1.00$ on the certified subset --- would complete the per-patient coordinate table.
  \item \textbf{A dedicated bootstrap and correlated-$G$ arm for the thin axes} (mania, substance), which
  currently inherit the joint nine-dimension certification rather than carrying their own.
  \item \textbf{A hurdle likelihood for \code{isf09a}} (the suicide-attempt count), the lone item-level
  mis-fit, should its count precision ever be needed; the suicidality factor itself is unaffected.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{From scientific validity to clinical utility}
Three boundaries are drawn by the data, not the method, and each names a defined next study.
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item \textbf{Incident-event outcomes.} Individual-level prognostic utility would require hard endpoints
  --- hospitalization, relapse, suicide attempt --- rather than the re-administered clinical scales
  available here; the present forecasts are of scale trajectories, not events.
  \item \textbf{Randomized or trial-arm treatment data.} Genuine treatment \emph{selection} --- which drug
  for which phenotype --- cannot be established from observational treatment-as-usual, however carefully
  adjusted; it requires a randomized or trial-arm study, for which the present work supplies the moderators
  to test and a deployable causal pipeline.
  \item \textbf{External validation.} Every result here is internally validated on a single baseline cohort;
  a clinical claim would require replication in an independent sample.
\end{itemize}
