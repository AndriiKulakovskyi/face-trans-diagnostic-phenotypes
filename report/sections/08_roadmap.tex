\section{Roadmap: the milestones ahead}
\label{sec:roadmap}

This document is a living artifact. \textbf{Milestones 1 and 2 are complete} (pending PI sign-off) --- the
measurement map and its stratification --- and are the foundation the remaining milestones build on. Each is a
separate milestone with its own acceptance gate, and none begins before the previous is locked. \textbf{M3 is
next.}

\milestonestrip{3}

\paragraph{M2 --- Validated strata.} \statuspill{facekr}{COMPLETE}\ \emph{(Section~\ref{sec:strata}).} On the
nine-dimension coordinates the transdiagnostic space is a \textbf{continuum, not biotypes}: eight extreme
phenotypes (every patient a soft blend), severity as the spine, distinct metabolic and inflammatory corners,
and a partition that cuts across DSM-5 (adjusted Rand index $\approx 0$) while describing the coordinates far
better than diagnosis ($\approx 21\%$ vs $\approx 5\%$ variance explained). Internal/descriptive validity only
--- the predictive and treatment head-to-heads are M4/M5.

\paragraph{M3 --- Temporal coherence.} \planned\ \textbf{The immediate next step.} Do the coordinates persist
across follow-up visits V1--V4?
The M1 map is scored forward onto later visits (fit-once, score-anyone; Section~\ref{sec:estimation}), and the
within-patient stability of each dimension is quantified. This is where invariant~I3 pays off: because the map
was fixed on baseline, later visits are an honest out-of-sample test of persistence rather than a fitting
target.

\paragraph{M4 --- Prognosis.} \planned\ Does a patient's position on the map carry incremental predictive value
\emph{beyond} diagnosis and overall severity for clinically meaningful outcomes? The biology$\perp$severity
finding (Section~\ref{sec:results}) is the substantive bet here: if biological load varies independently of
burden, biological strata can predict outcomes that a severity score alone misses. Evaluation will be a nested
head-to-head (diagnosis + severity vs.\ + dimensions), with the downstream cohort \emph{scored} from the
full-sample map, never driving the measurement fit.

\paragraph{M5 --- Treatment.} \planned\ Target-trial emulation, only if the data support it --- estimating
whether map position modifies treatment response. This is the most demanding milestone and the most
conditional; it will be specified only after M2--M4 establish that the strata are stable, persistent, and
prognostic.

\subsection{Milestones 1 and 2 are complete --- and the small follow-ons they leave}
The deliverables are all in hand: the certified nine-dimension map with its hardening chain
(Sections~\ref{sec:validation}--\ref{sec:scoring}) and prior$\to$posterior atlas; and, on those coordinates,
the validated transdiagnostic continuum --- eight extreme phenotypes and a soft tessellation, internally
validated and adjudicated against DSM-5 (Section~\ref{sec:strata}). What remains is a short, principled list of
follow-ons, none of which changes a conclusion: full-$N$ full-precision certification of the explicit-latent
block (the suicidality$\sim$developmental $\Phi$ coupling); full-$N$ projection of the non-Gaussian per-patient
scores; a separate bootstrap and correlated-$G$ arm for mania/substance (they already carry the 9-dim
cross-seed $\varphi$ $0.993$); and a hurdle likelihood for \code{isf09a} if its count precision is needed.
\textbf{PI sign-off on the adjudication, the atlas, and the strata findings locks M1\,$+$\,M2; then M3.}
