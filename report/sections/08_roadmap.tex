\section{Roadmap: the milestones ahead}
\label{sec:roadmap}

This document is a living artifact. Milestone~1 --- the measurement map --- is \textbf{complete} (pending PI
sign-off) and is the foundation the remaining milestones build on. Each is a separate milestone with its own
acceptance gate, and none begins before the previous is locked.

\milestonestrip{2}

\paragraph{M2 --- Validated strata.} \planned\ \textbf{The immediate next step.} Probabilistic decision regions
on the M1 coordinates: a segmentation of the \emph{nine}-dimension space into recurring, decision-relevant
subgroups, propagating the per-patient coordinate uncertainty from Section~\ref{sec:scoring} (a patient with one
observed cognition indicator is not treated as equally characterised as one with six). Validation will use
cohort/site composition, $\eta^2$, ARI against diagnosis, and stability under resampling --- with the explicit
guard that strata must not reduce to diagnosis, site, or missingness. The biology$\perp$$G$ finding is the
substantive bet: strata that use the biological axes can separate patients who look equally ill but differ
biologically.

\paragraph{M3 --- Temporal coherence.} \planned\ Do the coordinates persist across follow-up visits V1--V4?
The M1 map is scored forward onto later visits (fit-once, score-anyone; Section~\ref{sec:estimation}), and the
within-patient stability of each dimension is quantified. This is where invariant~I3 pays off: because the map
was fixed on baseline, later visits are an honest out-of-sample test of persistence rather than a fitting
target.

\paragraph{M4 --- Prognosis.} \planned\ Does a patient's position on the map carry incremental predictive value
\emph{beyond} diagnosis and overall severity for clinically meaningful outcomes? The biology$\perp$severity
finding (Section~\ref{sec:results}) is the substantive bet here: if biological load varies independently of
burden, biological strata can predict outcomes that a severity score alone misses. Evaluation will be a nested
head-to-head (diagnosis + severity vs.\ + dimensions), with the downstream cohort \emph{scored} from the
full-sample map, never driving the measurement fit.

\paragraph{M5 --- Treatment.} \planned\ Target-trial emulation, only if the data support it --- estimating
whether map position modifies treatment response. This is the most demanding milestone and the most
conditional; it will be specified only after M2--M4 establish that the strata are stable, persistent, and
prognostic.

\subsection{M1 is complete --- and the small follow-ons it leaves}
The M1 deliverables are all in hand: the certified nine-dimension map; the full hardening chain
(Section~\ref{sec:validation}); per-patient coordinates with reliability flags (Section~\ref{sec:scoring}); and
the centerpiece \emph{prior $\to$ posterior} atlas with the per-candidate verdicts of
Table~\ref{tab:adjudication}. What remains is a short, principled list of M2-prep follow-ons, none of which
changes a conclusion: full-$N$ full-precision certification of the explicit-latent block (the
suicidality$\sim$developmental $\Phi$ coupling, currently certified at the largest $N$ that mixes); full-$N$
projection of the non-Gaussian per-patient scores (a logistic/count conditional); a separate bootstrap and
correlated-$G$ arm for mania/substance (they already carry the 9-dim cross-seed $\varphi$ $0.993$); and a
hurdle likelihood for \code{isf09a} if its count precision is ever needed. \textbf{PI sign-off on the
adjudication and the atlas locks M1.}
