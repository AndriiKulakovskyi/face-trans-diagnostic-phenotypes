\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This program asked whether the harmonized baseline of three diagnostic cohorts --- bipolar disorder,
schizophrenia, and depression --- can be reorganized from a set of categories into a transdiagnostic,
biology-aware coordinate system that \emph{describes}, \emph{tracks}, \emph{forecasts}, and ultimately
\emph{guides} care better than the diagnostic labels themselves. It pursued that question under a discipline
that refuses to collapse its four layers (cohorts $\to$ dimensions $\to$ strata $\to$ prognosis/treatment) or to
manufacture certainty it has not earned: an observed-data likelihood with \emph{no imputation}, diagnosis held
as metadata rather than a feature, dimensions fixed on the baseline visit and only ever \emph{validated}
forward, and every estimate carrying its own uncertainty into the next. The answer is the three-pillar thesis
this report has defended, and it is best read by leading with what the work \emph{delivers}.

\textbf{Pillar~I --- dimensional analysis delivered a map the categories hide.} One global Bayesian
bifactor/ESEM on observed cells yields a nine-dimension structure --- a general functional-burden factor $G$ and
eight weakly-correlated specific axes --- that is real, identifiable, hardened across six adversarial validation
arms, transdiagnostic (adjusted Rand $\approx 0$ vs.\ the DSM-5 subtypes), and a \emph{tighter} description of
the patients than diagnosis. Its load-bearing result is a \emph{three-way} dissociation,
\textbf{biology $\perp$ symptoms $\perp$ severity}: metabolic and inflammatory load are the \emph{least}
severity-entangled domain in the map (metabolic$\sim$$G$ $+0.12$, inflammatory$\sim$$G$ $+0.07$, against $0.39$
for cognition and $0.42$ for sleep) \emph{and} sit at the $\approx 0.08$ off-diagonal floor against the symptom
axes, both legs surviving adjustment for medication, adiposity, and site. Two patients equally ill by a
clinician's global impression, and presenting with the same symptoms, can still differ sharply in biological
load: the dimensional reading surfaces a risk axis that neither the category, the severity scale, nor the
symptom profile can.

\textbf{Pillar~II --- archetypes made the map interpretable.} On these coordinates patients form not discrete
biotypes but a graded \emph{continuum} --- a single-Gaussian falsification null cannot be separated from the
real data (best silhouette $0.140$ vs.\ $0.141\pm0.002$) --- across which four stable soft archetypes mark
quasi-independent corners of biology, symptoms, and severity. The archetypes are what make a continuum usable:
they compress a nine-vector into a handful of clinically nameable weights, render the biology$\perp$severity
dissociation as two patients a clinician can point to (a biological corner and a non-biological severe corner,
\emph{equally ill, biologically opposite}), and supply a transdiagnostic patient-similarity tool. Where hard
groups are demanded, a nested $K$-family is exported with no privileged $K$ --- honestly, because a continuum
has none.

\textbf{Pillar~III --- operating in the latent space paid off.} Scored forward onto follow-up without
re-estimation, the measurement is invariant (all five backbone axes $\varphi\ge0.95$) and the geometry replays
as \emph{durable biology} (metabolic test--retest $\text{ICC}=0.91$) and \emph{moving symptoms}. A baseline
coordinate then \emph{forecasts} two-year functioning incrementally beyond diagnosis, severity, and the present
outcome (the archetype phenotype adds $\Delta\text{ELPD}=+59$, co-informative with DSM-5, $+$both $+67$), with
the biological corner carrying the worst functional prognosis ($27\%$ remission against $60\%$ at the low-burden
pole). The map is moreover a \emph{sufficient, structurally faithful} compression of the $143$ raw indicators,
and its prognostic carrier even \emph{survives} adjustment for the treatments patients received. The clinical
objects this yields are real and usable now: group-level stratification, continuous functional forecasting, and
a treatment-course monitoring atlas.

The honest reading separates two bars the field too often conflates, and the bounds are stated as plainly as the
gains. On \textbf{scientific validity} the program succeeds: the map is real, identifiable, stable,
transdiagnostic, a continuum rather than a set of biotypes, with biology the least severity-entangled domain and
a genuine, robustness-surviving prognostic signal for functioning. On \textbf{clinical utility in the strong
sense} it does not yet: the individual-level prognostic increment is small (the $+59$-ELPD group-level gain
collapses to a functional-remission $\Delta\text{AUC}$ of only $+0.011$, $0.745\to0.756$), and the map does not
moderate treatment in observational data (lithium in bipolar disorder, the cleanest contrast, is a well-powered
null; E-values $1.06$--$1.78$). Several of the program's most secure findings are therefore \emph{negative or
modest} --- no biotypes, no reliable treatment moderation, a group-level rather than individual prognostic gain
--- and they are presented as part of the contribution, not buried beneath the seductive numbers nor inflated
beyond what the data carry.

Each tension marks the exact edge of what the data support, and is carried with the claim rather than smoothed
over. The predictive object is a \emph{phenotype}, not an isolated axis: the forecast lives in the fuller
four-archetype representation --- biology read as a corner of the whole space --- not in a lone three-axis
``durable block'', which on its own is only ambiguous. The prognostic gain is \emph{group-level}, sharpening who
on average is heading where rather than calling an individual remission, and it is \emph{course-dependent},
carried by the episodic bipolar and depression patients and near-null in the baseline-saturated schizophrenia
cohort. Durability is cleanest in the \emph{variance} decomposition (the error-corrected trait/state ICCs); the
parallel geometry route is precision-confounded and is treated as corroboration. And the map's thinnest axis,
\emph{mania} (two indicators), is reliable-looking but prognostically and temporally uninformative, and is
flagged as such rather than over-read.

What the work establishes, then, is a transdiagnostic biological map that \textbf{complements} the diagnostic
system rather than replacing it: co-informative with DSM-5 prognostically, a tighter description of the
coordinates than diagnosis, and a continuous, uncertainty-aware representation of where a patient sits in
biology-and-severity space. The boundary is drawn by the data, not by the method, and the work names its own
exits (Chapter~\ref{sec:limitations}): individual-level clinical utility would require incident-event outcomes
rather than re-administered scales; treatment \emph{selection} would require randomized or trial-arm data rather
than treatment-as-usual --- a defined future study (M5b); and any clinical claim would require external
validation beyond this internally-validated baseline cohort. Each is a defined next study, and the present
program is the methods-and-feasibility foundation on which they can be built --- together with a reusable,
principled pipeline (no imputation, uncertainty propagated end-to-end, fixed-then-validated objects,
overlap-gated causal inference) for asking these questions without over-claiming.

\begin{keyresult}[The deliverable, stated plainly]
A stable, transdiagnostic biological map, made interpretable by a four-archetype geometry, whose durable
biological corner forecasts two-year functioning beyond diagnosis and severity at the group level, and that ---
on observational data --- does not yet guide treatment. Real scientific value, genuine group-level clinical
value, and an honest account of how far it reaches: a trustworthy map, and a marked road to the studies that
would carry it to the bedside.
\end{keyresult}
