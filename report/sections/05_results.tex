\section{Results: the M1 measurement map}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{The reported seven-dimension map}

general factor and six specific axes, estimated from each patient's observed cells with no imputation
(Table~\ref{tab:map}).

\begin{keyresult}[The dimensions are genuinely distinct axes]
The inter-dimension correlations are weak --- mean $|\Phi_{\text{off-diag}}|\approx 0.10$. The six specifics are
\emph{not} facets of one severity factor; the map carries real multidimensional information for stratification.
The only non-trivial couplings are clinically coherent: metabolic--inflammatory $0.19$ (immunometabolic),
suicidality--developmental $0.23$ (childhood adversity), and sleep's mild links to developmental ($0.19$) and
suicidality ($0.14$).
\end{keyresult}

The hybrid adjudication ran end to end: of the ten candidates the data \textbf{confirmed} six $+\,G$,
\textbf{split} candidate 5 into metabolic $+$ inflammatory, \textbf{rejected} anhedonia, and \textbf{dropped}
the three with no common indicators (impulsivity, negative symptoms, sensory). Depression/anxiety
(MADRS/QIDS/STAI) are \emph{cross-loading windows} onto $G$, not a dimension. The per-stage evidence follows.

\subsection{S1 --- the continuous core}
\label{sub:s1}
\certified\ A bifactor model on the $68$ continuous indicators homing on $G$ + the four continuous specifics, full $N$
($415{,}531$ observed cells): $\max\widehat{R}=1.010$, $\min\mathrm{ESS}=1{,}939$, $0$ divergences, no Heywood.
$G$ is anchored cleanly by functioning and global severity --- FAST $1.04$, EGF $0.69$, EQ-5D $0.58$, CGI-S
$0.54$ --- with subjective illness (\code{lvsbjind}) loading $\approx 0.01$: patients' subjective rating barely
tracks the objective burden axis. The load-bearing read of S1 concerns the bifactor loadings of biology on $G$:

\subsection{S2 --- inter-dimension $\Phi$ and the depression/anxiety windows}
\certified\ Deforming S1 into a correlated-factors ESEM (adding $\Phi$, freeing the MADRS/QIDS/STAI windows), warm-started
from the certified S1 posterior, on full $N$ ($J=71$, $434{,}765$ cells): $\max\widehat{R}=1.010$,
$\min\mathrm{ESS}=676$, $0$ divergences. Three results land.

\toprule

The strongest link, metabolic$\leftrightarrow$inflammatory $0.20$, is a modest immunometabolic coupling that
leaves the two clearly distinct --- supporting the hypothesised split. The depression/anxiety windows load
predominantly on $G$ (MADRS $0.80$, QIDS $0.77$, STAI $0.66$) with a smaller, sensible sleep side-loading
(QIDS $0.24$, STAI $0.21$): \emph{depression and anxiety severity are expressions of overall illness burden,
not a separate latent}. And S1 survives elaboration --- the biology$\perp G$ loadings are \emph{identical}
(metabolic $0.08$, inflammatory $0.07$), so the headline was not a bifactor/independence artefact.

\begin{methodsbox}[An identification finding, not just an engineering note]
Freeing the metabolic$\leftrightarrow$inflammatory mutual cross-loadings \emph{and} a free $\Phi_{me}$
parameterizes the same covariance two ways --- a rotational ridge (chains swung $\Phi_{me}$ from $-0.16$ to
$+0.46$) that also made the full-$N$ fit intractable. The metabolic/inflammatory shared variance is
identifiable as a \emph{factor correlation} ($\Phi\approx 0.20$), not as separable item-level bridges; the
mutual crosses are left at zero (the standard ESEM resolution) and $\Phi$ carries the association.
\end{methodsbox}

\subsection{S3 --- developmental-risk and mixed-likelihood suicidality}
\textbf{S3a (developmental-risk)} \certified\ on a random $N=4{,}000$ sub-sample (six factors;
$\widehat{R}=1.010$, $\mathrm{ESS}=832$, $0$ div). Developmental-risk is its own axis (mean primary $0.41$;
$\approx$ orthogonal to biology and $G$; weakly tied to sleep $+0.16$), and the continuous core is unchanged
from S2.

\textbf{S3b (mixed-likelihood suicidality)} \provisional. The binary/ordinal/count ISF indicators enter via the
explicit block coupled through $\Phi$; the suicidality factor is \emph{solidly identified} (each binary item
$\widehat{R}=1.00$, $\mathrm{ESS}$ $0.8$--$2.3$k), even though the global structural $\mathrm{ESS}$ is low
($58$) and $\widehat{R}=1.06$ --- the slow mixing is in the continuous cross-loadings and the
suicidality$\sim$developmental $\Phi$ cell, \emph{not} the suicidality block.

\subsection{S4 --- anhedonia}
\rejected\ Candidate 4 is thin (one dedicated indicator, \code{qids_anhedonia_interest}, BP/DR only; SZ has no QIDS).
Tested on the S3a map, it does not form a stable distinct factor: $\widehat{R}=1.54$, $\mathrm{ESS}=7$ at both
the smoke and the $N=4{,}000$ fair test, with a reflection/collapse instability across chains. When it forms it
is redundant --- its indicator loads $0.61$ on $G$ and the QIDS-total window loads $0.365$ onto it (and the
anhedonia anchor is a component of the QIDS total, so the two are near-collinear by construction). The rest of
the map is undisturbed. \textbf{Verdict: rejected as a standalone dimension} --- variance absorbed by $G$ + the
depression windows --- which is the methods-plan's anticipated outcome, and a result rather than a failure.

\subsection{S5 --- the global fit and the refined biology$\perp G$ verdict}
\provisional\ The single global model (all seven dimensions; continuous core marginalized, suicidality/developmental

continuous $+\,18$ non-Gaussian. It gives the reported $\Phi$ (Table~\ref{tab:s5phi}) and the dual-identification
test of the project's premise.

\begin{keyresult}[The load-bearing premise, honestly refined]
Tested under \emph{both} identifications: in the bifactor, biology's direct $G$-loadings are
$\approx 0$ (metabolic $0.08$, inflammatory $0.07$); in the correlated-$G$ sensitivity, the biology
\emph{factors} correlate $0.28$ (metabolic) / $0.14$ (inflammatory) with $G$ --- versus cognition $0.35$, sleep
$0.47$, developmental $0.28$. So biology is the \emph{least} severity-entangled domain ($\approx 92$--$98\%$ of
its variance independent of $G$) but \emph{not strictly orthogonal}. The exact claim is
\emph{``largely severity-independent,''} not ``orthogonal.'' Two equally-impaired patients can still differ
sharply in biological load --- biological strata remain worth drawing --- but the bifactor's
strict-orthogonality phrasing overstated it. This correction is the dual-identification test doing its job.
\end{keyresult}
