\section*{Relation to the companion paper}
This paper describes the measurement instrument. The clinical and biological
findings the instrument supports --- the transdiagnostic map's structure, the
immunometabolic axis, the archetype-level prognosis --- are reported in a companion
paper (in preparation). The two are designed to be read independently: the present
paper establishes that the map's coordinates, uncertainties and summaries are
trustworthy and efficiently obtainable; the companion paper interprets what they
reveal about severe mental illness.

\section*{Data availability}
The FACE database per-patient cohort data are confidential and governed by
FondaMental's data-access procedures, to which qualified researchers may apply. No
per-patient data are reproduced in this article. Aggregate parameters, derived
structure, the fitted loading and information tables, and all figure source values
are available in the project's regenerated aggregate outputs. The calibration and
adaptive-testing analyses use only the binary observed/missing pattern of the real
data (never any clinical value) together with model-generated synthetic responses.

\section*{Code availability}
The full analysis pipeline (data harmonization, the Bayesian measurement engine,
projection and scoring, the information/value-of-information accounting, and
archetypal analysis), the harmonized variable dictionary, and configuration files
are available at
\url{https://github.com/AndriiKulakovskyi/face-trans-diagnostic-phenotypes.git}.
The scripts that regenerate every figure in this paper, and the new
patient-adaptive, calibration and reconstruction analyses, are included. Every
reported quantity is reproducible from the numbered pipeline; per-patient data are
never required to regenerate the aggregate results and figures.
