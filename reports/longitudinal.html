FACE — discrete clustering is unstable and diagnosis-independent (negative result motivating the dimensional model)

This page is a negative result. The structure test shows the trans-diagnostic structure is dimensional, not discrete; here we force discrete k-means phenotypes and show they are neither temporally stable nor aligned to DSM-5 — i.e. slices of a continuum, not natural kinds. Assignment rule = a phenotype classifier trained on V0 (domain scores → V0 labels), 5-fold V0 accuracy 0.842, applied unchanged to each follow-up. DR excluded at V3 (attrition).

Comparison with the 7 DSM-5 subgroups

The data-driven phenotypes cut across DSM-5: the adjusted Rand index between the 7 enrolled DSM-5 subtypes and the V0 phenotypes is 0.006 (0 = independent, 1 = identical). Each phenotype draws from multiple diagnoses and each diagnosis spreads across phenotypes — the phenotypes are trans-diagnostic, not relabelled diagnoses.
